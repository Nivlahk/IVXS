What IVX Is

IVX is a browser-based programming language where every program renders as a live animated flowchart. IVX runs entirely in your browser and interacts with Google services on your behalf. There are no IVX servers that process or store your data.

IVX consists of two components with different licensing:

Open source — The IVX language itself: the interpreter, lexer, parser, and flowchart layout algorithm. Licensed under Apache 2.0. Free to use, modify, and redistribute under those terms.

Proprietary — The visual rendering layer: the SVG output, exported JSON and other collapsed representations, and the visual style of rendered flowcharts. These are owned by IVX and are not covered by the Apache license.
